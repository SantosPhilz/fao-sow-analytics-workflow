# Safe sharing guidance for this repository

## Keep public
- your own R scripts
- methods notes
- public-safe screenshots
- simulated or anonymized data
- high-level workflow descriptions

## Do not upload publicly unless approved
- internal FAO source datasets
- unpublished official visuals
- confidential review files
- country-level raw reporting files if restricted
- any material with internal comments or private identifiers

## Good public-safe approach
If someone opens this repository, they should be able to understand:
- the structure of the workflow,
- the tools used,
- the type of outputs produced,
- and your role in the analysis,

without gaining access to restricted source data.
